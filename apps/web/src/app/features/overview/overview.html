WORKSPACE OVERVIEW

A place for your team’s conversations.

Talk in shared channels, browse earlier messages and pick up the conversation after reconnecting.

Try the local demo

For an installation with the demo loaded, use the two local demo passwords supplied by the person running it.

  1. Sign in as alice@demo.example.test and open Demo Lounge.
  2. Open a private window or another browser, then sign in as bob@demo.example.test and open the same channel.
  3. Send a message and watch it arrive in both sessions. Load earlier messages to explore the history.

Sign in to the demo

Demo accounts and channels are shared. Other participants can see these conversations. Do not enter personal or confidential information. A demo reset clears accounts, sessions, channels and messages.
